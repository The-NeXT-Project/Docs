# Check-in and Invites

Two ways to earn on a site that has them enabled. Both are optional features the operator turns on, so not every site offers them.

## Daily check-in

If your dashboard has a **Daily check-in** card, pressing the button once a day grants a traffic reward. The card tells you the range before you press: some sites give a fixed amount, most draw a random amount between a minimum and a maximum.

Details that catch people out:

- One check-in per day. The card shows when you last checked in.
- Days do not accumulate. Missing yesterday does not give you two rewards today.
- Some sites require a verified email address, and some put a CAPTCHA on it.
- The reward is added to your traffic allowance and appears in **Billing** → **Traffic history**.

You can also check in from a linked Telegram or Discord bot with `/checkin`, without opening the site.

## Invite and earn

**Account** → **Invite and earn** holds your personal invite link and the record of what it has earned. The page states the current rules in words, because operators configure them differently — read those rather than assuming.

### The two kinds of reward

**On registration.** When someone signs up through your link, the site can give them a starting balance and give you a traffic reward. This happens once, at sign-up.

**On payment.** When someone you invited pays an invoice, you earn a percentage of it as a rebate, credited to your balance. This is the one that adds up.

### The caps

Rebates are capped, in one of two ways depending on how the operator set it up:

- **A count cap** — each person you invited can generate a rebate a fixed number of times. Their first three payments earn you something; the fourth does not.
- **An amount cap** — each person you invited can generate up to a fixed total, after which they stop earning you anything regardless of how much they spend.

Your invite page shows which cap applies and at what number, along with your total earned to date and a table of every rebate: who it came from, how much, and when.

### Using your balance

Rebates land in your account balance, which pays invoices like any other balance. There is no separate withdrawal — it is credit on this site.

### Resetting your link

**Reset** issues a new invite code. The old link stops working, including for anyone who has it saved. People who already signed up through it stay attributed to you; you keep earning from them.

## What does not earn

Two things worth stating plainly, because they are the usual questions:

- Inviting an account that never pays earns you the registration reward and nothing more.
- Creating accounts yourself to invite is visible in the record — same registration address, same payment method, no real usage — and is the reason sites shadow-ban. A shadow-banned account still connects but stops earning rebates and cannot buy anything, without being told why.
